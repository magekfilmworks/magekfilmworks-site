Multicam Live Production

Association meetings, corporate presentations, concerts, press conferences, educational events — delivered live to desktop and mobile, inside your organisation or out to several destinations at once. Every stream runs redundant: one primary, one backup. On-screen graphics handle Q&A, including questions pulled from social, and the whole thing is recorded for playback and on-demand.

ATEM Constellation · Ross Carbonite · Roland V-160HD · Panasonic AV-HS410 / 500 · YouTube · Facebook Live · X · Private embeddable player

HI-RES Screens & playback

ProAV and High-Resolution Switching

Large-format screen work for stages and ballrooms: high-resolution switching, blended widescreens, presentation and media playback. The same discipline that runs a broadcast feed, applied to the screens in the room.

Analog Way Aquilon · Barco Encore3 · PixelHue Q8, P80, P20 · Millumin · Mitti · Resolume Arena

CLOUD Remote crew

Cloud and REMI Production

Remote-integration workflows, so the show happens in the room and the production happens wherever the crew is. Useful when travel is the expensive part of the budget, or when the calendar puts two events on the same day.

REMI workflows · Remote switching · Distributed crew · Cloud review and delivery
