Privacy

What this site collects.

Short version: nothing, unless you write to us.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies. It stores nothing in your browser — no local storage, no session storage, no device identifiers. There is no analytics package, no advertising pixel and no third-party tracker of any kind. Nothing here follows you to another site.

What loads from elsewhere

Type and images are served from this site’s own domain, so simply reading a page contacts nobody but us. Two things reach outside, and only when you ask for them:

  • The map on the contact section loads from Google Maps, and only once you scroll far enough down the page to see it. Google may set cookies at that point, under their own privacy policy. Never scroll that far and no request is made.
  • The videos only load when you press play. One is on YouTube and uses their no-cookie player; until you click, nothing is requested from YouTube and nothing is recorded by them. The rest are served from our own AWS storage, which sets no cookies and involves no third party.

The project form

The form asks about your project so we can answer with something useful. It collects only what you type into it — your name, how to reach you, and what you tell us about the work. There are no hidden fields.

We use that information to reply to you and to scope the job. We do not sell it, rent it, or share it with anyone outside Magek Filmworks except where a project requires it and you have asked us to. If you would like us to delete what you sent, write to us and we will.

Getting in touch

Questions about any of this, or a request to see or delete what we hold, go to info@magekfilmworks.com.
